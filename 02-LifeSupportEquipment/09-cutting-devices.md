> A dive knife has a sharp fixed or folding blade and a sturdy handle. Many knife blades have both a smooth edge and a serrated edge. The serrated edge can cut through tough materials such as fishing lines, ropes, and nets. This makes a knife a practical tool for responding to underwater entanglements. A dive knife is also a general-purpose tool that can be used for prying, digging, or cutting vegetation.
