# Avoiding Panic

</p>

> Even if you’ve mastered the art of avoiding panic, it’s possible that the divers around you haven’t. You should keep an eye out for other divers panicking, whether they’re your official buddies or not.

<br>

<p align="left">
  <img src="https://github.com/thespcrewroy/MARS4740-ScientificDiving/blob/main/assets/6-7.jpg"/>
</p>

1. Completely fill your BC.
2. Calmly talk to your buddy from a distance
3. Instruct your buddy to establish positive buoyancy.
4. Approach your buddy and ditch his or her weights and inflate their BC
5. Help your buddy find the position where breathing is the most comfortable
6. If your panicked buddy grabs you, dive below the surface for them to let go
7. Never needlessly endanger yourself!
