# Value of a Dive Buddy

<p align="left">
  <img src="https://github.com/thespcrewroy/MARS4740-ScientificDiving/blob/main/assets/4-1.jpg""/>
</p>

> Your dive buddy might be your spouse, a friend or someone who is assigned to you as a buddy by a Divemaster on a boat. You always have the right to decline diving with someone with whom you do not feel comfortable. Trust your instincts regardless of who that intended buddy might be. Remember, your comfort and confidence in the open water is the most important factor of any dive.
