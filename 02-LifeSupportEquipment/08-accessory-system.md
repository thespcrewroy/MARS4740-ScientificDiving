> The dive light is very useful on any dive. As you learned in Section 1, the deeper we go the more we lose color due to the loss of the sun’s penetration. An artificial light allows you to see the real color of soft and hard corals, even at depth. So, a dive light can be used for several purposes, including day diving, night or limited visibility diving or exploring wrecks.
