> The only type of dive you can plan with dive tables is single-level, but most recreational dives are multi-level. Since multi-level dives with several depths cannot be planned with dive tables, one of the most welcome advantages of dive computers is that they respond to changing depths and are continually recalculating during a dive. The result for most dives is more time underwater without needing a decompression stop. Therefore, dive computers are designed for real divers, like you, who do repetitive dives on consecutive days.
