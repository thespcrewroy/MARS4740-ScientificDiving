> It’s essential to keep an accurate record of your personal info and dives. Your dive computer will do that, too, but the dive computer can only track certain things. Not all of the info the dive computer tracks will help you make your next dive even more fun and comfortable. What makes a dive enjoyable or not is knowing you have the right thermal protection to wear and the right amount of weight needed for a diversity of dives. The next time you go diving, you may not remember how much weight you wore and with what exposure suit unless you write it down.

> In addition to tracking your number of logged dives, your MySSI Logbook is an important resource of information for subsequent dives. It’s also a record of all the SSI programs you have taken. The more you do with SSI, the more recognition you will receive. For example, once you have taken four individual specialty courses of your choice and gotten certified, plus made 24 total dives, you will be awarded the SSI Advanced Specialty Diver recognition card free of charge. Go on a little further and add the SSI Diver Stress and Rescue and Deep Diver certifications, plus 50 total dives, and you will be awarded the SSI Master Diver certification.
