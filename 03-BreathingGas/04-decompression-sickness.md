> Rapid or uncontrolled ascents can have very serious consequences. A diver who ascends can create a high desaturation gradient for each of the inert gases that have been absorbed. This can result in supersaturation of the tissues and blood. The gases may separate out of solution to form bubbles which can create the condition known as decompression illness (DCI).

> If immediate medical help is not available, follow these general rules for all overexpansion injuries and decompression sickness: 1. Activate the Emergency Medical System (EMS) and comply with the directions regarding victim care and transport to a medical facility. 2. Treat for shock and monitor vital signs; 3. If qualified, administer oxygen; 4. If qualified, administer CPR, if necessary; and NEVER take a diver back underwater and attempt recompression. ALWAYS have an emergency plan before you dive! List the phone numbers and radio frequencies of the Coast Guard, lifeguard service, and other useful information in your MySSI Logbook.
