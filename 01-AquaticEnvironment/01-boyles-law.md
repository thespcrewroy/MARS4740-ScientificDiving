```
| Depth         | Absolute Pressure | Air Volume | Air Density |
|---------------|-------------------|------------|-------------|
| 0 ft (0 m)    | 1 ATA             | 1          | 1×          |
| 33 ft (10 m)  | 2 ATA             | 1/2        | 2×          |
| 66 ft (20 m)  | 3 ATA             | 1/3        | 3×          |
| 99 ft (30 m)  | 4 ATA             | 1/4        | 4×          |
```

> The diagram illustrates Boyle’s Law: as a diver descends, pressure increases and the volume of trapped air decreases. During ascent, pressure decreases and the air expands. The greatest proportional change occurs between 33 feet and the surface, which is why divers must ascend slowly and never hold their breath.
