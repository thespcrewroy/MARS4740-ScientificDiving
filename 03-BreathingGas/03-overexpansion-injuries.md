> In Pneumothorax, the second most serious lung overexpansion injury, air escapes from the lungs and moves between the covering of the lungs (pleural lining) and the rib cage. The expanding gas often collapses the lung and can put pressure on the heart.

> Divers should always be transferred to the nearest Emergency Department for stabilization and subsequently transferred to a recompression chamber. More often than not, chambers are not staffed 24 hours per day and only a handful are capable of providing the type of advanced life support needed for critically ill patients.
