> Contamination of the breathing medium can be caused by placing a gas compressor’s intake too near the exhaust manifold of a boat. It can also be caused by overheating, which causes the internal lubricants to burn and foul the gas. To prevent carbon monoxide poisoning during your dive, be sure to obtain pure gas from your SSI Training Center or a reputable diving operation.
