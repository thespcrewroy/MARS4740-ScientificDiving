1. Positive Buoyancy: An object that weighs less than the weight of the liquid
2. Negative Buoyancy: An object that weighs more than the weight of the liquid
3. Neutral Buoyancy: An object that weighs equal to the weight of the liquid. Neutral buoyancy is where you want to fill your air sacs up to at your desired point within the deep watery depths
